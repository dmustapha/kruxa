## NON-NEGOTIABLES
1. **No Solana mint for a launch that did not PASS, and the mint that is created has no authority left.** A Solana mint for a launch exists only after `settle` returned PASS; it is created once, in one ceremony session, with mint authority None, freeze authority None and no token extensions; `verify.sh` proves "this account is a mint owned by the classic Token program with 82 bytes", never just "account is null". - Test: sandbox: `commit_genesis` on an Open or Failed launch returns `R_GENESIS_STATE`; a second `commit_genesis` returns `R_GENESIS_ALREADY`; a GenesisPlan with an authority flag or extension flag wrong returns `R_GENESIS_AUTH`; devnet: Launch A mint passes `verify.sh` C4, C7, C8, C24 (exit 0); Launch B's address is absent and `verify.sh` C30 passes; `--tamper mint-authority`, `--tamper freeze-authority`, `--tamper extension` each exit 2 with `MISMATCH`. - Judge-attack: call `commit_genesis` on a failed or still-open trial; drop lamports on the empty address of a dissolved launch so a "null" check looks like a mint; ask the relayer to mint extra supply or keep an authority; claim the mint exists early. -> Defense: genesis methods are callable only by `GENESIS_RELAYER` and only when `LaunchStatus` is `Passed` (`R_GENESIS_STATE`, `R_GENESIS_ALREADY`); the relayer job state machine has no edge to `G_CREATED` before `PASS_SEEN`; the compiler hard-codes `SetAuthority(None)` after the last `MintTo` in the same transaction and has no authority parameter; `verify.sh` checks owner, size 82, authorities and supply conservation and exits 2 with `MISMATCH C<n>` on any difference. **Enforcement level: checked (F1).** NEAR cannot stop a Solana-side action by the relayer (detected, not prevented); under F0 (the NEAR contract is the only signer) this becomes structural. Do not describe it as structural until F0 is adopted and run.
2. **Every buyer can Walk until the walk deadline, and Walk cannot be blocked or redirected.** Walk removes the whole position from the verdict inputs synchronously, with no cross-contract call; the principal (after any relay fee already paid) returns to the buyer's own account (NEAR account, or own Intents account for Intents buyers); the destination is fixed by the Position. - Test: sandbox: after `walk`, `total_active` falls by the position, `active_funders` falls by one and the `book_hash` fold matches; `walk` at or after `walk_end` returns `R_WALK_CLOSED`; a caller with no position gets `R_NO_POSITION`; a failing payout leaves the verdict inputs unchanged and the refund retryable; under PAUSED, exits still work. - Judge-attack: creator, keeper or relayer tries to block an exit; Walk after the verdict; a third party triggers `claim_refund` to redirect funds; a refund transfer fails and strands money; a Phantom-only buyer has no NEAR key and cannot exit. -> Defense: `R_WALK_CLOSED`, `R_NO_POSITION`; `claim_refund` pays only to the destination stored in the Position; debit first, pay second, re-credit in a callback that never panics (`R_MATH` is the only math failure code); `walk_signed` verifies the exact message bytes (`R_SIG`, `R_NONCE`, `R_DEADLINE`, `R_SIGNED_MSG`); PAUSED keeps exits open and FROZEN expires after 7 days.
3. **The creator draws nothing before PASS, never more than the stream cap after PASS, and can only tighten terms.** Draw cap at time t is `floor(total x (t - pass_at) / duration)` and is zero at or before PASS; creator, platform and launch-fee accruals are zero on FAIL; after the trial opens, terms ratchet down or lengthen only; a creator or payee cannot buy their own launch. - Test: sandbox: "Withdraw all" after PASS reverts with `STREAM_CAP`; a draw on a failed launch is refused; property tests show `cap(t)` is monotonic and `cap(end) == total`; hostile presets (creator 60% allocation, zero window, creator take above 1.00% of volume, platform bucket not zero) return `R_ALLOC`, `R_WINDOW`, `R_CREATOR_TAKE`, `R_BUCKETS`; a ratchet that loosens a term returns `R_RATCHET`; a creator commit returns `R_SELF_BUY`. - Judge-attack: withdraw everything on day one; lower the stream length after PASS; open a launch with a 2.00% fee and 60% creator share and a 100% creator bucket; buy your own launch from the creator account or a payee; call `draw` before a stream exists. -> Defense: structural for the stream: the `Stream` row is created only inside `settle` on PASS, so a pre-PASS draw has nothing to draw (`R_WRONG_PHASE`; S1-C spells it `NOT_PASS`, alias [ASSUMED]); over-cap draws return `STREAM_CAP`; `R_STREAM`, `R_STREAM_INIT`, `R_CREATOR_TAKE` (exact integer check `total_fee_bps x creator_bps <= 1,000,000`), `R_CREATOR_SHARE`, `R_FEE_TOTAL`, `R_ALLOC`, `R_BUCKETS`, `R_VEST`, `R_RATCHET`, `R_SELF_BUY`; parameters are snapshotted into the Charter at `create_trial` so a later constant change cannot loosen an existing launch. Honesty limit: the contract caps the declared creator slice, not everything creator-controlled wallets can extract (see HONESTY RULES below).
4. **The verdict is a pure function of three recorded inputs and anyone can recompute it.** PASS needs total active commits at least the soft cap, active backers at least the minimum, and not cancelled; `settle` makes no cross-contract call; equality passes and one micro-USDC below fails. - Test: unit: `u_verdict_boundary_eq` PASS and `boundary-minus-one` FAIL(BELOW_SOFT_CAP); `kruxa-sim verdict --inputs fixtures/pass.json` prints PASS and `MATCH`; `verify.sh` C2 and C31 and C32 recompute verdict and `book_hash`; `--tamper spec-hash` and `--tamper root` exit 2; conservation sim `make sim` prints `conservation 50/50 ok`. - Judge-attack: front-run `settle`; call it before the deadline; flip the result with a Walk at the last block; break the verdict with an Intents outage or a solvency alert; ask the platform to override. -> Defense: `R_TOO_EARLY` before `walk_end`; `settle` is idempotent and returns the stored outcome; the Walk predicate and the `settle` predicate read the same block timestamp, so they cannot straddle (`R_WALK_CLOSED`); no admin method exists that changes a stored verdict; `verify.sh` exits 2 with `MISMATCH` when the recomputed inputs differ from the stored record.
5. **Money in the ledger is conserved, only the right callers can credit it, and caps hold.** Identities L1, L3, L4 and I2 to I5 hold after every mutation; only the USDC contract can call `ft_on_transfer` and only `intents.near` can call `mt_on_transfer`; the platform never pays a buyer's gas (buyers pay a quoted flat relay fee or submit the call themselves); caps are $1,000 per launch and $3,000 global in USDC and raising them is timelocked. - Test: sandbox adversarial suite: fake token calling `ft_on_transfer`, direct `mt_on_transfer`, callback starvation, reentrancy, donation inflation, over-cap commit, relay fee above ceiling, hex signer delivering to another address; `get_invariants` returns all true after each step; `check_solvency` flags `credited > mt_balance_of`. - Judge-attack: pretend to be the token contract; call the Intents callback directly; send USDC to inflate "backing"; commit above the wallet or launch cap; inflate the relay fee; make the platform pay gas for a buyer; drain Intents-held funds through the Verifier's force powers. -> Defense: `R_UNEXPECTED_TOKEN`, `R_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `R_SOLVENCY`, `R_MATH`, `R_CAP_ACCOUNT` (alias `R_WALLET_CAP`), `R_CAP_LAUNCH`, `R_CAP_GLOBAL`, `R_CAP_HOLDERS` [DERIVED], `R_MIN_CONTRIBUTION` (alias `R_MIN_BUY`, checked on the amount net of the relay fee), `R_RELAY_FEE` [DERIVED], `R_DELIVER`, `R_PAUSED`; the verdict counts only credited amounts; the Verifier's force-withdraw powers are detected by `reconcile_intents` and disclosed, not prevented.
6. **Mainnet hygiene and authority.** Mainnet is the product target; testnet and devnet are rehearsal plus the no-funds judge path (CO-3). No token or mint of any kind exists on mainnet before the brand token ($KRUXA) launches, and the deployed mainnet core enforces it: `create_trial` returns `R_CREATOR_GATE` for every account but the owner until the owner opens the capped beta (one-way, after $KRUXA genesis succeeds and BLK-1 is closed); every rehearsal runs on NEAR testnet and Solana devnet; nothing is signed, spent, deployed to mainnet, posted, pushed, made public or submitted without Dami's per-action confirmation; throwaway keys stay in the scratchpad or env vars and never in the repo; the trusted-relayer credit path is not built unless S3 and S4 both fail and Dami agrees. - Test: `npm run guard` in `warroom/signed-tests` prints PASS (no token, mint, Metaplex or authority code outside the devnet-only T4 script, which asserts the devnet genesis hash before building and sending); repo secret scan finds no private key or seed; the cargo feature `relayer-intake` is OFF in every build; mainnet deploy and signing scripts refuse to run without an explicit confirmation flag [ASSUMED]. - Judge-attack: find a Kruxa-created token or mint on mainnet before the brand launch; find a key in the repo history; find a post, push or submission that Dami did not approve; find a hidden relayer credit path. -> Defense: guard failure (`npm run guard` exits non-zero and prints FAIL) blocks the build; the per-action confirmation log is part of the gate GR-11; `R_CAP_LAUNCH` and `R_CAP_GLOBAL` bound mainnet exposure; the Genesis Round runs only after the Nov 10 go/no-go (a real check, never skipped) and only with Dami's confirmation of each step. CO-3 test: sandbox `create_trial` from a non-owner account on the mainnet profile returns `R_CREATOR_GATE`.
7. **State shown to judges is real, labelled, and recomputable.** Every pre-run launch is labelled "opened earlier" with tx hashes; every non-live element carries one of the labels `SANDBOX`, `DRY-RUN`, `DRY QUOTE`, `RECORDED`, `TESTNET`, `DEVNET` or `MAINNET`; the disclosures are always visible: no independent audit, single-party reserve in the demo, factory token publisher-updatable, Intents Verifier force powers, relay fee not refunded, NEAR-side token exists from the start of the trial. - Test: README lint script finds each label where a non-live element appears and finds the disclosure strings on `/charter` and in the README risk box; `verify.sh --evidence FILE` prints "RECORDED, not live" in its output; `verify.sh` returns exit 3 for pending and exit 4 for infrastructure failure, never a success code. - Judge-attack: a cached verdict, a stored success response, a fabricated count, an unlabelled recording, a success animation with no call behind it, a hidden testnet. -> Defense: live paths read chain state; recorded paths are hashed evidence labelled `RECORDED`; infrastructure disagreement is exit 4 and is never converted to a pass; the seed script creates only real testnet/devnet state; nothing in the app returns a success without a call.
8. **(CO-3) The payout recipient is fixed once a trial opens.** The recipient of stream draws, creator fee claims and allocation claims defaults to the account that signs `create_trial`, may be a separate account chosen at creation, appears in the Charter and `charter_hash`, and cannot change after the trial opens. ### HONESTY RULES: the creator maximum (finding S1-C 9.1, decision 12)
1. The contract enforces the **declared** creator slice: total fee at most 2.00%, creator share at most 60%, exact integer check so the declared slice is at most 1.00% of volume.
2. A creator who also runs undeclared staking or referral wallets can capture parts of the holder slice (holders range 10% to 30%) and the referral slice (up to 20%). With platform share at least 15% and holders plus LP at least 20% and LP at least 5%, everything creator-controlled wallets can extract is at most 80% of the fee: 1.60% of volume at the 2.00% maximum fee.
3. Wash trading loses the creator at least 20% of the fees paid for every permitted configuration.
3b. (CO-5) At the ruled default split (50/15/15/10/10) creator-controlled wallets can capture the creator slice (50) plus holder (15) plus referral (10) = 75% of the fee; the contract-bound worst case across all legal presets stays 80%. Say 75% for the default and 80% for the bound, never one for the other.
4. The `declared_wallets` view lists creator, payees, recovery accounts and the allocation beneficiary. It is not sybil resistance and is never sold as such.
5. The fee layer ships in Wave 6. Until then the README says there is no market and the fee terms are validated and stored but inert.
6. (CO-3) The fee layer ships pre-freeze as Market-lite only if the M-gate (Nov 11 EOD) is green; otherwise the README says there is no mainnet market and the fee terms are stored but inert. Exemption lists: none by default; any list must be public, in the Charter, fixed at open and capped (NON-NEGOTIABLE 9).
