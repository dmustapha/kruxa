Verbatim paragraph from `research/launchpad-deep-research.md` in the Kruxa docs repo (the full file is not shipped here). The figures are third-party and self-reported; this repo does not recompute them. `scripts/verify-claims.sh C-07` checks only that this paragraph is present and carries the preprint citation and the vendor caveat.

Every study agrees that most launches fail, but the headline rate swings with the definition. Solidus Labs (a vendor report, self-reported) classed about 98.6% of pump.fun tokens as rugs or pump-and-dumps ([Solidus](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)). A Feb 2026 preprint on 41,470 migrated tokens found **84.13%** high-risk, defined as falling below 0.3x of migration price within 20 minutes, with bundled accounts holding **36.5% of supply** at migration ([MemeTrans](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480)). A rule-based detector flagged 76.42% of 100,063 tokens listed on Orca, Raydium and Meteora in H1 2025 ([SolRugDetector](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24625v1)). The strictest definition, deployer-funded same-block sniping, gives only about 1.75% of issuance, though 87% of those snipes were profitable and over half exited in under a minute ([Pine Analytics](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/exit-liquidity-machines); independent analyst, unverified). Of 655,770 tokens in one month of Sept 2025, only **0.63%** graduated ([arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1)). My reading: use 84-98% as "default outcome is loss" and the 1.75% and 36.5% figures for the narrower sniper and bundle problems.
